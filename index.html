Definitions & formulas
Gross scheduled rent
Total monthly rent for all units × 12, before vacancy.
Vacancy
Income lost to unoccupied units or uncollected rent, modeled as a percentage of gross rent.
Maintenance
Recurring repairs and upkeep. The annual allowance is gross rent × maintenance percentage.
Capital expenditures (CapEx)
Major replacements such as roofs, HVAC, and windows. The reserve sets aside cash for future work; it is separate from routine maintenance.
Net operating income (NOI)
Gross rent − vacancy − operating expenses (tax, insurance, HOA, maintenance, management, and other expenses). Excludes mortgage payments and CapEx.
Cap rate
Conventional: NOI ÷ purchase price. Your decision metric: (NOI − CapEx reserve) ÷ purchase price.
Debt service & P&I
Mortgage principal and interest (P&I). Annual debt service is the monthly P&I payment × 12. Taxes and insurance are separate.
Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR)
Conventional: NOI ÷ annual debt service. Your decision metric: (NOI − CapEx reserve) ÷ annual debt service. A ratio of 1.30 means $1.30 of available income per $1 of debt payments. No debt means the ratio does not apply.
Cash flow
NOI − CapEx reserve − mortgage debt service. Monthly cash flow per unit is annual cash flow ÷ 12 ÷ number of units.
Initial cash invested
Down payment + closing costs + initial renovation + other upfront cash and reserves.
Cash-on-cash return
Annual pre-tax cash flow after reserves ÷ initial cash invested. It cannot be evaluated when initial cash is zero.
Stress test
Recalculates the deal with more conservative expense assumptions, keeping rent, fixed costs, and financing unchanged.
INVEST / REVIEW / REJECT
INVEST meets every pass target and stress rule. REVIEW meets all minimums but misses a pass target. REJECT violates a review floor, minimum expense assumption, or stress rule.
Pre-tax rental screening. Appreciation, principal paydown, sale proceeds, and tax benefits are excluded. Verify rent, expenses, property condition, and loan terms before purchasing.
